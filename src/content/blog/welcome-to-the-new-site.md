Net Intellect has looked after businesses across Albury-Wodonga, the Riverina and northeast Victoria since 1996. Our website hadn't kept up, so we've rebuilt it from the ground up.

## What's new

- **Clearer services.** Each service now has its own page explaining what's included, who it suits and what you can expect: SupportNet Managed IT, cybersecurity, Microsoft 365, phones and mobile plans, networking, broadband, and backup and disaster recovery.
- **A free Cybersecurity Review.** Book it from any page. We check for staff passwords exposed online, how well your email and Microsoft 365 are protected, and what your systems show to the internet. Nothing is installed on your computers, and you get a plain-English report with clear priorities.
- **What our clients say.** Some of the organisations we've worked with for years, in their own words.
- **Faster and simpler.** The site loads quickly on any device and works well on a phone.

## What's gone

Our online shop has closed. If you're after an SSL certificate or anything else we used to sell there, [get in touch](/contact) and we'll sort it out for you.

## What's next

We'll be posting practical advice here for businesses like yours: security, Microsoft 365, and getting more out of the technology you already have. If there's something you'd like us to cover, let us know.
